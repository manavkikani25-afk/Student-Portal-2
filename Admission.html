Undergraduate Engineering Admissions

Join Gujarat's premier engineering institute through merit counseling and institutional quotas.

Application Process • Step-by-Step

Step 1

Online Registration

Register on the ACPC Gujarat portal or university direct admission window with basic academic details.

Step 2

Merit Assessment

Merit rank calculated based on 50% 12th Board PCM percentile and 50% GUJCET or JEE Main score.

Step 3

Seat Allotment

Participate in mock counseling rounds and select preferred FTE branch choices.

Step 4

Campus Reporting

Report to FTE Changa campus with original certificates, pay token fees, and receive welcome kit.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed Gujarat Board (GSEB) or CBSE/ICSE equivalent 10+2 examination in Science stream.
  • Mandatory subjects: Physics and Mathematics, along with Chemistry, Biotechnology, or Computer Science.
  • Minimum aggregate marks: 45% (General category) and 40% (Reserved categories - SC/ST/SEBC).
  • Valid scorecard in GUJCET 2026 or JEE (Main) 2026.
