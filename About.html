About Charotar University & FTE

Pioneering world-class engineering education, research breakthroughs, and societal advancement.

Our Vision

To become a dynamic global university through innovation, knowledge creation, and holistic student development, producing ethically grounded engineers and technological innovators who solve global challenges.

🌟

Our Mission

Deliver rigorous academic frameworks, foster interdisciplinary research, nurture industry-academia partnerships, and empower future engineering leaders with lifelong critical inquiry and entrepreneurial mindsets.

Academic Excellence & Accreditations

The Faculty of Technology & Engineering (FTE) stands at the forefront of contemporary technical training in Gujarat. Our programs combine solid theoretical foundations with intensive hands-on laboratory engagements.

🖊 NAAC 'A+' Grade

Accredited with highest academic credentials reflecting stringent teaching-learning standards.

🤖 15+ Advanced Labs

Equipped with high-performance GPUs, IoT rigs, cybersecurity testbeds, and robotics suites.

🤝 100+ Corporate MoUs

Active industry collaborations providing guaranteed internships, mentorship, and live projects.
