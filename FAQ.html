Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common queries regarding academics, portal security, and campus services.

As per CHARUSAT university academic regulations, every student must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance in theory lectures and 85% in laboratory practical sessions to be eligible to sit for the end-semester university examinations.

Navigate to the Study Material section from the main navigation bar. Resources are organized by subject code (e.g. ITUE203 for Web Development Frameworks). You can download verified PDFs directly without any credentials or paywall.

Student Portal implements the industry-standard split-token architecture (selector & SHA-256 hashed validator). The database stores only the one-way cryptographic hash of the token, ensuring that even in the unlikely event of database access, user login tokens cannot be reversed.

Log in using your registered student credentials. Go to Campus Events or view available events directly on your Student Dashboard. Click the "Register Now" button next to any upcoming event to reserve your seat.

Ensure that your mobile number consists of exactly 10 digits starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9, and your password contains at least 8 characters. If you still encounter issues, visit the Helpdesk page to submit a support ticket to our system administrators.

You must bring original copies of your Class 10 and 12 marksheets, school leaving certificate (LC), GUJCET/JEE scorecard, caste certificate (if applicable), and 4 passport-size photographs to the administrative admissions office.
